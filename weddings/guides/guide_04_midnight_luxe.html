Section Five
Pro Tips & Notes

Things Nobody Tells You

Venue and photographer availability should drive every other booking decision.
Reserve 5–10% of your total budget as an unspoken contingency fund.
Catering quotes never include service charges and gratuity — confirm the final all-in cost.
Add 30 minutes of buffer to every photography window — you will run late.
Mail invitations 8 weeks ahead; 10–12 weeks for destination events.
Eat before your wedding morning hair and makeup appointment.
A trusted non-wedding-party point person on the day is invaluable.
Your vows deserve more time than you think they do. Begin early.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

Are you available on [YOUR WEDDING DATE]?
How many events do you work per weekend?
What is your contingency plan for illness?
May we see a complete portfolio or past event examples?
What is precisely included in this package?
What are your overtime rates?
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Do you require a vendor meal at the reception?
Who specifically will be at our wedding?
