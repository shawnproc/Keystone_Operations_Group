Pro Tips & Notes

Things Nobody Tells You

Book your venue and photographer first — all other bookings follow their calendar.
Set aside 5–10% of your total budget for surprises and overlooked expenses.
Catering quotes never include service fees or gratuity — always ask for the all-in number.
Build a 30-minute buffer into every photography block — you will always be behind.
Mail invitations 8 weeks in advance; 10–12 weeks for destination events.
Eat a real breakfast on your wedding morning — you will forget otherwise.
Designate a non-wedding-party friend as your day-of logistics person.
Start your vows far earlier than feels comfortable.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

Are you available on [YOUR WEDDING DATE]?
How many weddings do you take per weekend?
What is your backup plan for illness or emergency?
May we see a full sample or complete portfolio?
What exactly is included in your package?
What are your overtime fees?
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Do you need a vendor meal on the day?
Who specifically will be at our wedding?
