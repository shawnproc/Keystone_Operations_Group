# Keystone Operations Group — Services Overview

## What We Actually Do
We set up automated communication systems for service businesses.
When a customer calls, texts, emails, or books — the right message goes
out automatically, at the right time, without the business owner lifting a finger.

That's it. Simple.

---

## What Is a Business Process Setup (Flow)?
A flow is an automated communication template built around one trigger
in the business. You build it once — it runs automatically for every
customer that hits that trigger, forever.

**Simple Flow — $49**
Single trigger → single message
- Missed call → auto text reply
- Payment received → receipt confirmation
- Technician on the way → "en route" text
- Job completed → thank you message
- Bad review posted → owner alert

**Medium Flow — $79**
Trigger → 2-3 message sequence
- New lead → instant reply → 24hr follow-up → 48hr follow-up
- Job booked → confirmation → day before reminder → day of reminder
- Invoice sent → 3 day reminder → 7 day reminder
- Job completed → review request → 3 day follow-up if no review

**Complex Flow — $99**
Multi-step, conditional logic, or multiple channels
- Full lead nurture sequence (call, text, email combined)
- Re-engagement campaign (30, 60, 90 day touchpoints)
- Full onboarding sequence (day 1, 3, 7, 30 messages)
- Seasonal campaign with conditional branching
