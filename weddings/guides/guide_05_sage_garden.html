Pro Tips & Notes

Things Nobody Tells You

Book venue and photographer first — all other vendors work around their schedule.
Hold back 5–10% of your budget for unexpected expenses.
Catering quotes rarely include service fees and gratuity — always confirm the all-in cost.
Add a 30-minute buffer to every photography session — you will always run behind.
Mail invitations 8 weeks out; 10–12 weeks for destination events.
Eat a real breakfast on your wedding morning — you will forget otherwise.
Assign a non-wedding-party friend as your day-of logistics person.
Start writing your vows earlier than you think necessary.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

Are you available on [YOUR WEDDING DATE]?
How many events do you work per weekend?
What is your emergency backup plan?
May we see a complete portfolio or sample event?
What is included in your base package?
What are your overtime rates?
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Do you require a vendor meal at the reception?
Who specifically will be at our wedding?
