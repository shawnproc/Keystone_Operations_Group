Common Questions

Quick Answers

Are you a property management company?

No. We provide operations and administrative support — maintenance intake, vendor scheduling, reporting, and document tracking. You stay the landlord in charge of all financial and legal decisions.

What does my tenant actually experience?

Tenants submit maintenance requests through a simple intake channel we set up for your properties. They get acknowledgment and status updates. From their side, there's a clear line to report issues — and someone actually follows up.

I'm out of state. Can you still help?

Yes — remote and out-of-state landlords are one of our strongest use cases. Everything runs through digital channels. You get reports and notifications on anything needing your approval without needing to be there in person.

Can you work with my existing vendors?

Yes — that's usually the preference. You keep the vendors you trust, we handle the scheduling and follow-up. If you need vendors you don't have, we can source and vet options for you.

Do I still talk to my tenants directly?

On decisions that need you — yes. Anything requiring your approval comes to you directly. Day-to-day maintenance coordination we handle without pulling you in every time.

What if I want to cancel?

Everything is month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days notice, no penalties. We'll hand off all records cleanly. We're confident it won't come to that.
