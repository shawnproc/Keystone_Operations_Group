Pro Tips & Notes

Things Nobody Tells You

Book venue and photographer before everything else — their calendars drive every other booking.
Set aside 5–10% of your budget as a hidden contingency fund.
Catering per-head quotes exclude service fees and gratuity — get the real all-in number.
Build a 30-minute buffer into every photography block — you will always run behind.
Mail invitations 8 weeks out; 10–12 for destination events.
Eat a real meal on your wedding morning — you will be too busy otherwise.
Assign a non-wedding-party friend to manage day-of logistics.
Start your vow writing much earlier than feels necessary.
