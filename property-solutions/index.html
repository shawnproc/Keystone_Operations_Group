Common Questions

Quick Answers

Do you collect rent or sign leases?

No. Our property operations support stays on the admin and coordination side — maintenance intake, vendor scheduling, reporting, and document tracking. You remain the landlord in charge of all financial and legal decisions.

What does my tenant actually experience?

Tenants submit requests through a simple intake channel we set up for your properties. They get acknowledgment and status updates. From their side, they have a clear line to report issues — and someone actually follows up.

I'm out of state. Can you still help?

Yes — remote and out-of-state landlords are one of our strongest use cases. Everything runs through digital channels. You get reports and notifications on anything needing your approval without needing to be there in person.

How does HOA financial reporting work?

Board members get monthly financial statements — income, expenses, reserve balance, and delinquency report. You also have live access to your community's financials through your board portal at any time.

Can you work with my existing vendors?

Yes — that's usually the preference. You keep the vendors you trust, we handle the scheduling and follow-up. If you need vendors you don't have, we can source and vet them for you.

What if I want to cancel?

Everything is month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days notice, no penalties. We'll hand off all records cleanly. We're confident it won't come to that.
