## Niche Strategy — Emergency & After-Hours Service Businesses

The sharpest positioning for Keystone is businesses that receive calls after hours,
on weekends, or during emergencies — where missing that call means losing the job
entirely to whoever answers first. These owners can't be at their phone 24/7.
That's exactly the gap Keystone fills.

**Core Pitch for This Niche:**
> "You're losing jobs at night. We fix that."

## Core Business Model

Keystone Operations Group sells automated communication systems to small
service businesses. One-time setup fee + monthly support plan. No contracts,
cancel anytime.

**Target client:** Owner-operator service businesses with 1-5 technicians.
Not large companies with full staff and existing infrastructure.

## How to Find Prospects

- **Warm conversations** — business owners you meet naturally. Once you're
  in the mindset, you'll keep running into them. These are warmer than any
  scrape or cold list.
- Google Maps — search "[industry] [city]" and filter for 15-75 reviews
- Angi / Thumbtack — contractors actively spending money to grow already
  understand the value of more leads
- Facebook local business groups
- Nextdoor — service providers advertising there are almost always owner-operators

### OutScraper (Bulk Prospecting)

OutScraper scrapes Google Maps and exports a spreadsheet with business name,
phone, website, address, and review count — ready to work from.

**Recommended filter settings:**
- Category: [target trade — HVAC, plumbing, pest control, etc.]
- Location: [target city or radius]
- Reviews: 10 to 75
- Has phone: Yes
- Rating: 3.5 to 4.4

The 3.5-4.4 rating sweet spot is key. Businesses rated 4.5+ are usually
already dialed in. Under 3.5 have deeper problems than automation can fix.
The middle is almost always an owner-operator doing decent work with no system.

## Revenue Stream 2 — Rank and Rent

### What It Is
Build a website targeting a specific local service niche, rank it on Google
through SEO, then rent the leads or the site to a real local business for
a monthly fee. Once ranked, largely passive income.

### The Model
- Build a neutral, generic site (no real business name — e.g. "NoVA HVAC Pros")
- Rank it through local SEO (see below)
- Rent it to a local business for $500-$2,000/mo
- They receive the leads, you collect the rent
- Eventually sell the site outright as a ranked asset

### Key Rules
- Keep completely separate from Keystone Operations Group brand
- Different business name, domain, phone number, address
- No public connection to KOS — revenue flows to you privately
- KOS stays clean and premium; rank-and-rent sites can be scrappier

### How to Rank a Local Site

**On-Page SEO:**
- Target specific keywords (e.g. "emergency HVAC repair Manassas VA")
- Proper page structure — title tags, headers, meta descriptions
- Fast load speed, mobile-friendly

**Citations (NAP consistency):**
- Submit to 30-50 directories: Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Alignable,
  Chamber of Commerce sites, industry directories
- NAP = Name, Address, Phone — must be identical everywhere
- Tools: BrightLocal, Moz Local, Whitespark

**Backlinks:**
- Local directory listings (baseline)
- Guest posts on small business / industry blogs
- HARO (Connectively) — get quoted as an expert, earn a link
- Complementary business partnerships

**Google Business Profile:**
- Critical for local map pack (top 3 listings above organic results)
- Optimize fully — category, description, photos, posts

### Timeline
- Competitive niches: 3-9 months to rank
- Less competitive markets / smaller cities: faster
- Front end is real work — not passive until it ranks

### Selling the Site
Before handoff:
1. Swap business name to buyer's real name
2. Update phone to their real number
3. Update address to their location
4. Update all citations across directories to their NAP
5. Transfer domain or point to their hosting

**What you're actually selling:** The Google ranking, domain authority,
established citation footprint, and proven lead flow — not the placeholder
brand name.

**Lease-to-own approach:** Rent first, prove value, offer the buyout.
If they've been getting 20 leads a month for 6 months, selling is easy.
