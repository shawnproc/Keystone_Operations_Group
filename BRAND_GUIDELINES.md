## Logo

- Always use the official `logo.png` file
- Do not recreate, redraw, or approximate the logo
- Minimum size: 120px wide (digital)
- Clear space: leave breathing room equal to the height of the "K" on all sides
- On dark backgrounds: use the white/inverted version (CSS: `filter: brightness(0) invert(1)`)
- On light backgrounds: use the full-color version
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo

## Voice & Tone

**We are:**
- Plain-spoken — no jargon, no buzzwords
- Confident — we know what we do and it works
- Practical — focused on real results for real businesses
- Human — not robotic, not overly corporate

**We are not:**
- A tech company talking down to clients
- An "AI company" (we use automation tools, we don't lead with AI)
- Salesy or pushy
- Vague

**Word choices:**
- Say "automation" not "AI-powered workflows"
- Say "flows" not "sequences" or "pipelines" (externally)
- Say "Monthly Support Plan" not "retainer"
- Say "business process setup" not "workflow buildout"
- Say "cancel anytime" not "flexible subscription terms"

**Sentence style:** Short. Direct. No fluff.

## Document Standards

- All internal documents use Markdown (.md)
- Dates formatted as: Month YYYY (e.g., June 2026)
- Headers use Title Case
- Status tracked with: ✅ Complete · 🔄 In Progress · ⬜ Not Started · 🔒 Blocked

---

## What We Do Not Do

- No phone calls as part of the service model
- No year-long contracts
- No locking clients in — they stay because it works
- No managing clients' social media
- No cold calling on behalf of clients
- No building websites for clients
