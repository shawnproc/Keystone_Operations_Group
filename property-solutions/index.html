Common Questions

HOA Management FAQ

How long does it take to switch management companies?

Most communities complete the transition within 30 days. We coordinate directly with your previous management company to request records, notify vendors, and set up your new portal — your board does very little heavy lifting.

What happens if there's an emergency at 2am?

We have an after-hours emergency line staffed around the clock. Your residents call one number, we assess the situation, dispatch a vetted vendor, and notify your board — all before morning.

Can residents access their account and community info?

Yes. Every community gets a resident-facing portal where owners can pay dues, submit maintenance requests, view community documents, and see announcements — all in one place.

How does financial reporting work?

Board members get monthly financial statements — income, expenses, reserve balance, and delinquency report. You also have live read access to your community's financial records through the board portal at any time.

Do you manage the vendors or do we keep ours?

Your call. If you have vendors you trust, we'll work with them. If you want us to bring our vetted network in, we'll competitively bid the work and manage those relationships going forward.

What if the board wants to end the contract?

We're month-to-month, so you can cancel with 30 days notice. We'll coordinate an orderly transition of all records and vendor contacts to whoever comes next. No penalties, no games.
