# Keystone Operations Group — Demo Video Guide

## Overview
Three short demo videos — one per pricing tier — embedded on the pricing
page. Each shows a realistic, real-world walkthrough of what that tier
looks like from signup through daily operation. Screen recording style,
real phone receiving messages, on-screen captions (no voiceover needed).

Target length: under 90 seconds per video.

---

## What You Need Before Recording
- GHL test sub-account with dummy flows set up
- A real phone to receive demo texts/emails
- Loom (free at loom.com) for screen recording
- A second device to show the phone receiving messages

---

## Starter Tier Demo

**Narrative theme:** "While you're on the job, your business is still responding."

**What to show:**
1. A new lead texts in from a fake customer
2. Within seconds — auto-reply hits their phone
3. Lead books a job — confirmation sent automatically
4. Fast forward to the day before — reminder goes out, no one touched it
5. Incoming call goes missed — customer gets an auto-text within seconds

**On-screen captions to use:**
- "New lead comes in..."
- "Auto-reply sent in under 60 seconds"
- "Job booked — confirmation sent automatically"
- "Day before reminder — no action needed"
- "Missed a call? They already got a text."
- "This is Starter."

---

## Growth Tier Demo

**Narrative theme:** "Your follow-up game is now on autopilot."

**What to show:**
1. Quick flash of Starter features running in background (5 seconds)
2. Job finishes → 2 hours later review request text goes out automatically
3. Customer hasn't booked in 60 days → check-in text fires automatically
4. Invoice sent → unpaid 3 days later → reminder goes out on its own
5. Simple monthly report showing jobs completed, reviews collected, response rate

**On-screen captions to use:**
- "Job done — review request sent automatically 2 hours later"
- "Haven't heard from them in 60 days? They just got a text."
- "Invoice unpaid? Reminder sent. Automatically."
- "Your monthly snapshot — no report pulling needed"
- "This is Growth."

## Enterprise Tier Demo

**Narrative theme:** "Your whole operation running without you managing it."

**What to show:**
1. Quick flash of Growth features (5 seconds)
2. New technician assigned to a job → they get notified automatically
3. Bad review comes in → you get an internal alert immediately
4. New customer signs up → welcome sequence kicks off (show day 1, day 3, day 7 emails)
5. Spring season — entire customer list gets a seasonal campaign automatically
6. Dashboard view showing all activity across the business at a glance

**On-screen captions to use:**
- "New job assigned — technician notified automatically"
- "Bad review? You're alerted before you even see it."
- "New customer? 7-day welcome sequence starts immediately."
- "Seasonal campaign — sent to your entire list, automatically"
- "Everything, in one place."
- "This is Enterprise."

## Recording Tips
- Use a clean, simple phone background (no clutter)
- Show real timestamps on the messages so it feels authentic
- Keep transitions fast — cut dead air
- Add subtle zoom on the phone screen when messages come in
- Use Loom's drawing tool to highlight key moments
- Export at 1080p minimum
- Name files: demo-starter.mp4 / demo-growth.mp4 / demo-enterprise.mp4

---

## Where Videos Live on the Website
- Embedded directly on the pricing page above each tier card
- Autoplay on hover, muted, looped
- Full screen option available
- Mobile-friendly (vertical crop if needed)
