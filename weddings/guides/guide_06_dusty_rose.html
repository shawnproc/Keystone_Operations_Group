Section Five
Pro Tips & Notes

Things Nobody Tells You

Book venue and photographer first — all other vendors schedule around them.
Keep 5–10% of your budget in reserve for surprises.
Catering quotes almost never include service fees and gratuity.
Build a 30-minute buffer into every photography window.
Mail invitations 8 weeks out; 10–12 for destination weddings.
Eat something substantial on your wedding morning.
Have a non-wedding-party friend manage day-of logistics.
Start your vows earlier than feels comfortable.
