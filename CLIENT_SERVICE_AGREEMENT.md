## 1. Services to Be Provided

Keystone agrees to provide the following based on the Client's selected plan:

**Starter Plan**
- 1 Business Process Setup (automated communication flow)
- Automated appointment confirmation and reminder
- Missed call auto-text reply
- Email support
- Free fixes for any system issues

**Growth Plan**
- Everything in Starter
- Up to 3 Business Process Setups
- Automated review requests
- Re-engagement campaign
- Invoice and payment reminder flows
- Monthly performance report
- Priority email support
- Free fixes for any system issues

**Enterprise Plan**
- Everything in Growth
- Up to 8 Business Process Setups
- Full software and tool integration
- Custom reporting dashboard
- Onboarding and post-job sequences
- Seasonal and retention campaigns
- Internal team notification flows
- Free fixes for any system issues

Additional flows and add-ons may be added at any time at the rates
listed on the Keystone pricing page. Each addition becomes a recurring
line item on the Client's monthly bill once activated.

## 2. Payment Terms

### Setup Fee
Client agrees to pay a one-time setup fee at the time of signup.
Setup fees are non-refundable once work has begun.

### Monthly Support Plan
Client agrees to pay the applicable monthly fee on a recurring basis.
Billing begins on the date of signup and repeats on the same date
each month via automatic charge through Stripe.

### Additional Flows
Additional flows requested after initial setup will be billed at
the applicable rate and added as a recurring line item to the monthly
subscription upon activation. Client will be notified of the updated
monthly total before the next billing cycle.

### Late / Failed Payments
If a payment fails, Stripe will automatically retry. If payment cannot
be collected within 7 days of the due date, services may be suspended
until payment is received.

## 3. Onboarding Process

Following payment, Client agrees to:
- Complete the onboarding intake form within 5 business days
- Provide accurate and complete information about their business,
  tools, and communication needs
- Respond to any clarifying questions within 2 business days

Keystone will begin building the Client's automation system within
1 business day of receiving a completed intake form. Build times:
- Starter: 1 business day
- Growth: 2-3 business days
- Enterprise: 5-7 business days

## 4. Support & Maintenance

Keystone agrees to:
- Maintain all active automations in working order

  at no additional charge
- Notify the Client when a fix has been applied
- Respond to support inquiries within 2 business days

## 5. Cancellation Terms

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time.

**Client cancellation:**
- Client must notify Keystone by email at info@keystoneoperationsgroup.net
- Service remains active through the end of the current billing period
- Monthly fees are non-refundable
- All automations will be deactivated at end of billing period
- Client portal access will be revoked

**Keystone cancellation:**
- Keystone reserves the right to terminate service for violation of
  these Terms, non-payment, or misuse of the system
- Client will be given 7 days notice except in cases of serious violation
