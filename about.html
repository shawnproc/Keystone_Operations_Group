About Us

Built to Deliver.
Every Time.

Veteran-owned and operated out of Fredericksburg, Virginia.

Keystone Operations Group is a logistics company based in Fredericksburg, Virginia. We provide same-day courier and delivery services to commercial clients and select residential clients across the state. If something needs to get somewhere, we get it there — on time, every time.

We are veteran-owned and operated. That means something here. The same values that define military service — discipline, accountability, and follow-through — define how we run this company. Our clients don’t need to chase us down for updates or wonder if we’ll show up. We handle it.

We work primarily with commercial clients who need a dependable logistics partner they can count on without micromanaging. We also serve select residential clients who need the same level of professionalism. If reliability matters to you, we should talk.
