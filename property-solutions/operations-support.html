Common Questions

Property Operations FAQ

Are you a property management company?

No. We provide operations and administrative support. We don't collect rent, sign leases, negotiate on your behalf, or show units. You stay the landlord in charge — we handle the coordination work that eats up your time.

What does the tenant experience look like?

Tenants submit maintenance requests through a simple intake form or line we set up for your properties. They get acknowledgment and status updates. From their perspective, they have a clear channel to report issues — and someone actually follows up.

Do I still talk to my tenants directly?

On decisions that need you — yes. Anything requiring your approval (repairs above a threshold, access authorization, lease-related items) comes to you. Day-to-day maintenance coordination we handle without pulling you in.

What if I already have a property manager?

If your property manager is handling everything well, you probably don't need us. But if your PM is overloaded and you need additional coordination support, we work alongside existing management teams as a back-office layer.

Can you work with my existing vendors?

Yes — and that's usually the preference. You keep the vendor relationships you trust, we handle the scheduling and follow-up. If you need vendors you don't have, we can source and vet options for you.

I'm out of state. How does that work?

Remote landlords are one of our strongest use cases. Everything runs through digital channels — we coordinate locally on your behalf. You get reports, notifications on anything needing your decision, and full visibility without needing to be physically present.
