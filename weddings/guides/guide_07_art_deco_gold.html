Section Five
Pro Tips & Notes

Things Nobody Tells You

Book venue and photographer first — all other vendors follow their availability.
Keep 5–10% of your budget as an unspoken contingency.
Catering quotes exclude service charges and gratuity — confirm the all-in total.
Schedule a 30-minute photography buffer — you will always run behind.
Mail invitations 8 weeks out; 10–12 for destination weddings.
Eat a proper meal on your wedding morning.
Assign a non-party-member friend to manage logistics on the day.
Begin your vows earlier than you think necessary.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

Are you available on [YOUR WEDDING DATE]?
How many events do you work per weekend?
What is your contingency plan for illness?
May we see a complete portfolio?
What is included in your package?
What are your overtime rates?
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Do you require a vendor meal at the reception?
Who specifically will be at our wedding?
