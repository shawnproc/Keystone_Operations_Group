# Keystone Operations Group — Services Overview

## What We Actually Do
We set up automated communication systems for service businesses.
When a customer calls, texts, emails, or books — the right message goes
out automatically, at the right time, without the business owner lifting a finger.

That's it. Simple.

---

## What Is a Business Process Setup?
A single automated communication flow built around one trigger in the business.

Examples:
- New lead comes in → automatic text reply sent within 60 seconds
- Job is scheduled → confirmation email + day-before reminder sent automatically
- Job is completed → follow-up text asking for a review sent automatically
- Customer hasn't booked in 90 days → re-engagement text sent automatically
- Invoice is sent → automatic payment reminder if not paid in X days

Each one of those is ONE Business Process Setup.
