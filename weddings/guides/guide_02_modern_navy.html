Pro Tips & Notes
Insider wisdom and space to think

Things Nobody Tells You

Book your venue and photographer first — everything else depends on their availability.
Keep 5–10% of your budget in reserve for unexpected costs.
Catering per-head quotes rarely include service charges and gratuity — clarify upfront.
Build a 30-minute buffer into your photography timeline — you will run late.
Mail invitations 8 weeks out; 10–12 weeks for destination weddings.
Eat a real meal on your wedding morning. You will thank yourself later.
Assign a non-wedding-party point person to manage logistics on the day.
Start writing your vows much earlier than feels necessary.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

Are you available on [YOUR WEDDING DATE]?
How many weddings do you book per weekend?
What is your emergency backup plan?
Can we see a full portfolio or sample event?
What is included in the base package?
What are your overtime and additional rates?
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Do you require a vendor meal at the reception?
Who specifically will be on-site on our wedding day?
