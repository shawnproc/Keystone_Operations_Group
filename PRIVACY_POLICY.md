# Privacy Policy
**Keystone Operations Group LLC**
Last Updated: June 2026

---

This Privacy Policy explains how Keystone Operations Group LLC ("Keystone",
"we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when
you use our website or services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### From Website Visitors
- Name and email address (via contact form)
- Business name and area of interest (via contact form)
- IP address and browser data (via standard web analytics)

### From Clients
- Business owner name and contact information
- Business name, type, and operational details
- Software and tools currently in use
- Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we do not store
  card details)
- Communication preferences and workflow details provided during onboarding

### Automatically Collected
- Website usage data (pages visited, time on site)
- Device and browser type
- Referring website

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
- Process payments and manage your subscription
- Build and maintain your automation workflows
- Send service-related emails (confirmations, receipts, flow notifications)
- Send follow-up communications if you have expressed interest in our services
- Improve our website and services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third
parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our business:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Formspree | Contact form submission handling |

| GoHighLevel | CRM, automation, and client portal |

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they

---

## 4. Email Communications

By submitting our contact form or signing up for a service, you agree
to receive:
- Service-related emails (receipts, confirmations, flow notifications)
- Follow-up emails if you have inquired about our services

You may opt out of non-essential emails at any time by replying to
any email with "unsubscribe" in the subject line.

We comply with the CAN-SPAM Act. All emails include our business
name and a way to opt out.

---

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information including:
- SSL encryption on our website (HTTPS)
- Secure payment processing via Stripe (PCI compliant)
- Limited internal access to client data

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot
guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Active client data is retained for the duration of your subscription
- Upon cancellation, data is archived for 90 days then permanently deleted
- Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months
- Payment records are retained as required by law

---

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@keystoneoperationsgroup.net.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify
active clients by email at least 14 days before material changes
take effect. The updated policy will always be available on our website.
