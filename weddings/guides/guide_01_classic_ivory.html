Section Five
Pro Tips & Notes

Things Nobody Tells You

Book your venue and photographer first — everything else follows their availability.
Allocate 5–10% of your budget to a contingency fund for unexpected expenses.
The catering cost per head is always higher than the initial quote once you add service charges and gratuity.
Ask your photographer for a "getting ready" buffer — you'll always run behind schedule.
Send your invitations 8 weeks in advance; 10 weeks for destination weddings.
Eat something on your wedding morning — you will forget otherwise.
Assign a trusted non-wedding-party friend to manage day-of logistics so your family can enjoy the day.
Write your vows earlier than you think you need to. Way earlier.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

Are you available on [YOUR WEDDING DATE]?
How many weddings do you do per weekend / per day?
What is your backup plan if you are ill or have an emergency?
Can we see a full wedding sample / portfolio?
What is included in your base package?
What are your overtime rates?
When is the final payment due and what is your cancellation policy?
Do you require a meal at the reception?
Who specifically will be there on our wedding day?
